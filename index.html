SettleKit US Healthcare Gap Planner for Newcomers

SettleKit is a prototype planning experience for immigrants, international students, and workers moving to the United States. It helps users explore possible delays before employer or university health coverage begins, review risk exposure, and prepare questions before arrival.

SettleKit does not provide medical, legal, financial, or insurance advice. Coverage rules, eligibility, costs, and effective dates must be verified with licensed professionals, insurers, employers, universities, and official government sources.
